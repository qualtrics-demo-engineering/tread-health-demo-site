Common questions

How do I transfer a prescription to Tread Pharmacy?

Call us or use MyPortal with your pharmacy’s name and phone number. We will handle the rest.

Can I get a 90-day supply?

Often yes, if your provider and insurance allow it. Ask your pharmacist about options.

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most plans. See our Accepted Insurance page or call to check.

Can I talk to a pharmacist about side effects?

Absolutely. Pharmacists are available during pharmacy hours, in person or by phone.
