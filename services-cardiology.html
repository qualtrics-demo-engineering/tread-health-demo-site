What we treat

Our cardiology team diagnoses and treats conditions affecting the heart and blood vessels, combining advanced imaging and testing with a personalized approach to long-term heart health.

Coronary artery disease & heart attack care

Diagnosis, treatment, and a plan to protect your heart.

Arrhythmia & heart rhythm disorders

Palpitations and irregular rhythms, tested and treated.

Heart failure management

Ongoing care and tune-ups that help you feel your best.

Preventive cardiology & risk screening

Know your numbers and what to do about them.

Heart imaging & stress testing

Echo, stress tests, and monitoring, often in one visit.

Cardiac rehab

Guided exercise and support after a heart event.

What to expect

We explain every result in plain language, and we never rush the conversation.

Your consultation

New patients usually begin with a conversation, an EKG, and a risk assessment.

Tests, if needed

Imaging or a stress test helps us see the full picture. Most are done on site.

Your plan

Our team builds a treatment plan tailored to your heart health goals, whether that means lifestyle changes, medication, or advanced procedures.

Follow-up that fits

Check in by video, phone, or MyPortal. Rehab and support are there if you need them.

Common questions

Do I need a referral to see a cardiologist?

Many patients are referred by their primary care doctor, but you can also schedule directly. Check with your insurance plan if you are unsure.

What is an EKG?

An EKG records your heart’s electrical activity using small stickers on your chest. It is quick, painless, and takes just a few minutes.

How should I prepare for a stress test?

We will send you instructions ahead of time. In general, wear comfortable clothes and shoes and bring your medication list.

Can I see my results online?

Yes. Most results and visit summaries appear in MyPortal, and your doctor will talk them through with you.
