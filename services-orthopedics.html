Common questions

Do I need an X-ray before my visit?

Not necessarily. If you have recent images, bring them. If not, we can usually take X-rays on site.

Will I need surgery?

Many conditions improve without surgery. Your doctor will talk through non-surgical options first and explain when surgery is worth considering.

Can I walk in for a new injury?

For sprains and minor injuries, Urgent Care can see you without an appointment and refer you to Orthopedics if needed.

How long does recovery take?

It depends on the injury and your goals. Your care team will give you a realistic timeline and check in along the way.
