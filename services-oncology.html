Common questions

Can I bring someone with me to appointments?

Yes. Family and friends are welcome at most visits, and you can join by phone or video if they cannot be there in person.

Can I get a second opinion?

Of course. Your navigator can help you request records and arrange a visit.

How do I know if a clinical trial is right for me?

Your doctor can tell you about trials that may fit your situation and explain the risks and benefits. Joining a trial is always your choice.

What kind of support is there for my family?

Our social workers and counselors can help with questions, practical needs, and emotional support for the whole family.
