Visitor Guidelines

Everything you need to plan a visit, from hours and parking to what to bring for someone staying with us.

The quick version

Hours and rules vary by unit, so check the details below before you go.

8am to 8pmGeneral visiting hours on most inpatient units.
Two at a timeUp to two visitors per patient in most rooms.
Check in firstStop at the Welcome Desk with a photo ID for a visitor badge.
Kids welcomeChildren are welcome with an adult who stays with them.

Guidelines by area

Tap an area to see what to know before you visit.

General inpatient units

Visiting hours are 8am to 8pm. Please check in at the Welcome Desk, wear your visitor badge, and wash your hands when you enter and leave the room. Quiet hours begin at 8pm so patients can rest.

Intensive care

Visits are arranged with the care team so patients can rest and receive care. Please call the unit before you come. Family may be invited in for rounds and key conversations.

Pediatrics

Parents and guardians are welcome 24 hours a day. Siblings and other visitors are welcome during general hours. Our child life team can help siblings feel comfortable.

Emergency department

Because space is limited, one support person may accompany a patient in the treatment area. Other family and friends are welcome in the waiting room, and our team will give updates.

Behavioral health

Visits happen at scheduled times so patients can focus on their care. Please call ahead. Staff will explain what you can bring.

Infusion & oncology

Many infusion visits welcome one support person, and you can often join by phone or video. Check with the care team for your unit.

Outpatient clinics (Tread Clinic and Tread North)

You are welcome to bring a family member or friend to appointments. Please let the front desk know if you need extra seating, an interpreter, or any accessibility support.

Staying healthy together

A few small habits keep patients, visitors, and staff safe.

Feeling sick? Visit another day.

If you have a fever, cough, stomach bug, or other symptoms, please wait until you feel better. A phone or video call is a great way to say hello in the meantime. Masks may be requested in some areas, and we will provide one.

Getting here and getting around

Parking

A parking garage at Tread Medical Center, and free parking at Tread Clinic and Tread North. See locations.

The Tread Trail

Follow the colorful footprints on the floor to find your way to clinics, the Wellness Hub, and the exits.

Accessibility

Wheelchairs at every entrance, accessible parking, and volunteers in blue vests to help you get around.

Take a break at the Wellness Hub

Waiting is hard. The Tread Wellness Hub in our atrium has comfy seats, tea and coffee, a kids’ play corner, and a Gratitude Wall where you can leave a note for someone who made your day better.
