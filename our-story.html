Our Story

It started with a nurse, a black bag, and a lot of walking. Here is how a neighborhood health project grew into Tread Health.

Since 1924Built by neighborsStill walking

one step at a time

A hundred years, one step at a time

Follow the footprints. (This story is fictional and created for demonstration purposes.)

1924

It started with a pair of walking shoes.

Visiting nurse Eleanor Tread walked the streets of Metro City with a worn black bag and a lot of patience, caring for neighbors in their own kitchens. When the neighborhood held a bake sale to build a real hospital, Tread Health Hospital opened with twenty beds and a very big front porch.

1956

A new wing and a very loud ambulance.

A growing city needed more room. Tread added a modern wing, a proper emergency entrance, and the town’s first ambulance, which had a siren the neighbors still talk about.

1974

Health happens outside the hospital, too.

The first Tread Trail walks began as a Saturday-morning stroll. Neighborhood clinics, vaccine days, and the very first volunteer vests followed. The idea stuck: meet people where they live.

1991

Exam rooms with animals on the walls.

Tread Kids opened with painted woodland murals, a toy chest in every room, and one firm rule: every visit ends with a sticker.

2008

Room to breathe.

Tread Medical Center grew a glass atrium, a healing garden, and the Tread Wellness Hub, a place to take a break, sip tea, and leave a note on the Gratitude Wall.

2015

MyPortal says hello.

Results, messages, and appointments moved into one place, and the front desk finally got a break from sticky notes.

2021

Care that comes to you.

Video visits made it possible to see a Tread provider from the couch, the car, or the kitchen table, whichever was closest.

2026

Three places, one big neighborhood.

Tread Medical Center, Tread North, and Tread Clinic now care for neighbors across the region, with hundreds of volunteers, thousands of stickers, and still plenty of walking.

What we believe

Three simple ideas have guided every chapter.

Listen first

Every good plan starts with understanding what matters to the person in front of us.

Keep moving

Health is a path, not a destination. We walk it with you, one step at a time.

Everyone belongs

Every neighbor deserves care that is kind, clear, and close to home.
