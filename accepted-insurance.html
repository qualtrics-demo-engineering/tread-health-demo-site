Common questions

What if my plan is not listed?

Call us at (555) 010-2000. Plans change often, and we may still be able to see you or help you find the right option.

Do I need a referral?

It depends on your plan. HMO plans often require a referral for specialists, while PPO plans usually do not. Your insurer can confirm.

What does out-of-network mean?

It means we do not have a contract with your plan. You may still be able to see us, but costs can be higher. Ask us before you schedule.

Will I get a surprise bill?

We work to make costs clear up front. If you have questions about a bill, our billing team is happy to help, and you can review charges any time in MyPortal.

Questions about coverage?

Our billing team answers calls Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm.
