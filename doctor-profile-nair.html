About Dr. Nair

Dr. Priya Nair is a family-focused primary care physician at Tread Clinic. She loves the detective work of primary care: connecting small clues like sleep, stress, and family history into a plan that fits real life.

Dr. Nair is known for clear, jargon-free explanations and for always asking one question first: “What matters most to you?” Her team offers quick visits for new patients and walk-in lab draws on site.
