What to expect

Our goal is for kids to leave a little braver than they came in.

Common questions

When should my baby have their first visit?

Most newborns are seen within a few days of coming home. Call us and we will help you schedule it.

Can parents stay in the room?

Yes. Parents and guardians are welcome at every visit, and we will help make the visit comfortable for the whole family.

What if my child is nervous about shots?

Our nurses use distraction, comfort positions, and a lot of encouragement. Tell us what helps your child and we will do it.

Do you do sports physicals?

Yes. Bring any forms with you, or ask us to fill out your school or team paperwork at the visit.
