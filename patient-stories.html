Maya and the footprints that led her to the right room

Maya was nervous about her first shot at a new clinic. Then she spotted the colorful footprints on the floor and decided to follow them, hopping the whole way to her exam room.

Dr. Alvarez let Maya listen to her own heartbeat first, introduced a stuffed fox named Biscuit, and let Maya choose the sticker. “She asked if she could come back tomorrow,” her dad says.

Maya, age 7PediatricsTread Clinic

Walter’s plan to dance at his granddaughter’s wedding

Walter had a goal: to dance at his granddaughter’s wedding. His knee had other ideas. At Tread North, Dr. Osei started with a simple question about what Walter wanted to get back to doing.

A therapy plan, a few months of patience, and a lot of encouragement from his care team later, Walter led the first dance. “I had a plan, and everyone knew it,” he says.

Walter, age 68OrthopedicsTread North

Rosa and a team that knew her name

When Rosa began treatment, the hardest part was feeling like she had to explain herself over and over. Her nurse navigator changed that. She learned Rosa’s story, kept everyone on the same page, and answered every call.

Rosa now volunteers on the Gratitude Wall, where she helps new patients write their first note. “I never felt like a chart,” she says.

Rosa, age 54OncologyTread Medical Center
