Urgent Care

Walk-in treatment for minor injuries and illness when it cannot wait for an appointment. For emergencies, call 911.

Walk-ins welcomeOn-site X-ray & labEvenings & Saturdays
Schedule an Appointment Find a Location
walk right in

What we treat

No appointment needed. We treat the bumps, bugs, and sprains that cannot wait until your doctor has an opening.

Sprains & minor fractures

X-ray and splinting on site.

Cuts & minor burns

Cleaning, stitches, and care instructions.

Colds, flu & fever

Testing and treatment so you can rest and recover.

Ear, sinus & throat infections

Quick exams and the right treatment.

Rashes & insect bites

Look-overs and relief for itchy, puzzling skin.

Stomach bugs & dehydration

Care and fluids when you are not feeling right.

When to go to the emergency room instead

Call 911 or go to the ER for chest pain or pressure, trouble breathing, signs of a stroke (face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech), severe bleeding, or a serious head injury.

What to expect

Wait times vary through the day. Checking in online can help you hold your place in line.

Check in

Online before you leave home, or at the front desk when you arrive.

Quick triage

A nurse checks your vitals and asks what is going on, so the most urgent needs are seen first.

See a provider

An exam, tests or X-ray if needed, and a clear explanation.

Head home with a plan

Instructions, prescriptions sent to your pharmacy, and follow-up if you need it.

Common questions

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome at our urgent care locations. You can check in online to save your spot.

What if I need more care than urgent care can give?

Our team will let you know and help you get to the right place, including our emergency department at Tread Medical Center.

Will you send my visit to my regular doctor?

Yes. With your permission, your visit summary goes to your Tread doctor and appears in MyPortal.

Does insurance cover urgent care?

Most plans do. See our Accepted Insurance page or call us before your visit.
