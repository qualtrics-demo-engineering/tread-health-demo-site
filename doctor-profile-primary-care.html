About Dr. Chen

Dr. Maria Chen is a primary care physician at Tread Health's Downtown Campus, where she has practiced for over a decade. She focuses on preventive medicine and building long-term relationships with her patients, believing that the best care comes from truly knowing the person in front of her.

Dr. Chen sees patients of all ages for annual physicals, chronic condition management, and everyday health concerns, and is known for taking extra time to explain treatment options clearly.
